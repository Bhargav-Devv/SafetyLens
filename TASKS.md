## Phase 2 — Indian data  ⛔ CANNOT BE BUILT AS WRITTEN

**TASK-200 resolved 2026-09-20. See `docs/findings_indian_data.md` and ADR-020.**

The narratives exist and are mandatory — Form 18 under the Factories Act 1948 carries a free-text
description of the accident and its causes, filed within 4 hours for a fatality. They are filed with
**state** Inspectorates of Factories and **published nowhere**. What India publishes is
*"Industrial Injuries in Factories"*: aggregate counts by state and year. No records, no text.

The blocker is a publication decision, not language, not technology, and not effort.

- [x] TASK-200 **Resolved: no public source exists.** Verified against data.gov.in and the Form 18
      specification. This is the finding, not a failure to search

- [x] TASK-308 Scope routing + title-leads ranking (ADR-016). **Recall unchanged on held-out — the

- [x] TASK-311 **Closed: not implementable** (ADR-023). All 28 public-roadway OIICS events fall

- [x] TASK-312 **Split and resolved** (ADR-022). Administrative sections dropped from the index —

**Note.** TASK-300 came first for a reason, and it paid off immediately: the per-group breakdown
showed Transportation at 1.000 and Fires at 0.000, which named the actual defect (generic section
titles and procedural standards) rather than leaving "TF-IDF is weak" as the diagnosis.

**TASK-310 mattered exactly as much as it looked.** The scope-routing change raised dev recall@3 from
0.364 to 0.424 — a clean, publishable-looking 6-point gain. Held-out recall was **identical before and
after**. Without the second set that number ships, and nothing in the output would have shown it was
noise.

**All three sets are now spent.** TASK-311, TASK-312 and TASK-302b all need a fourth set.
Measuring twice on the same data is how the discipline quietly stops working.

**And it cuts both ways.** ADR-016's dev gain was noise and was discarded. ADR-018 looked on dev like
a *loss* at rank 1 and turned out to be +13 points on unseen data. The rule is not "trust the lower
number" — it is that thirty cases cannot tell you which way a change went.
